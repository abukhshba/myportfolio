Case Study

Enterprise CRM Platform

Customer Relationship Management

Laravel Filament GraphQL MySQL

Overview

This Enterprise CRM platform goes beyond standard customer relationship management by integrating comprehensive Human Resources, Payroll, and Performance Management modules into a single, cohesive ecosystem for large organizations.

Business Problem

The client faced significant inefficiencies managing sales pipelines and employee performance across disjointed tools. They needed a custom solution that inherently understood the link between their sales reps' CRM activities and their HR performance metrics.

Solution

Developed a robust Filament-powered dashboard that unifies CRM and HR. Engineered a highly dynamic schema to handle complex hierarchical organizational structures, dynamic payroll calculations, and advanced lead assignment algorithms.
