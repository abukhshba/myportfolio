Case Study

Buongo ERP

Enterprise Resource Planning System

Laravel Filament REST APIs MySQL

Overview

Buongo ERP is a large-scale, enterprise-grade resource planning platform designed to centralize and automate complex business operations. It acts as the backbone for organizational processes, integrating multiple departments into a single, cohesive system.

Business Problem

The organization was struggling with data silos across isolated systems for HR, Finance, and Sales. They needed a unified, highly secure, and permission-driven platform that could handle thousands of daily transactions and provide real-time analytics.

My Role

I led the backend architecture and development, designing a modular monolithic system. I implemented complex role-based access control (RBAC), developed the accounting core, and built REST APIs for external system integrations.

Technical Challenges & Solutions

Challenge: Financial Ledger Integrity

Ensuring 100% accuracy and ACID compliance for high-frequency double-entry accounting transactions across concurrent users.

Solution

Implemented rigorous database transactions, row-level locking (Pessimistic Locking), and a specialized event-driven architecture using Laravel Queues to safely process financial entries asynchronously without blocking the UI.
