Case Study

Alfehris

Industry & Trade, Verified Across the Middle East

Laravel 13 GraphQL (Lighthouse) Filament v5 MySQL Meilisearch Cloudflare R2 Pest
Visit alfehris.com

Overview

Alfehris is a bilingual (Arabic / English) B2B directory that connects buyers with verified factories across the Middle East. Buyers search by product, sector and location. Factories manage their own profiles and can bid on keywords to appear as sponsored results. The Laravel backend is the single source of truth: it serves a Next.js web front end through a GraphQL API and runs two separate Filament panels, one for platform staff and one for factory owners.

Business Problem

Industrial buyers in the region had no reliable, searchable place to find verified manufacturers. The data was scattered, mostly in Arabic and inconsistently spelled. The platform also needed a revenue model that didn't hurt organic search quality: factories pay for visibility, and buyers can still trust what they see.

My Role

As a Backend Engineer on the team, I worked on the Laravel backend: the GraphQL API, the Filament admin and factory panels, and the business logic behind search, the ads system and SEO.

Key Features

Keyword-Bid Ad Auction

Factories bid on keywords. Sponsored results are ranked by an auction, billed per click, tracked through signed redirect URLs and moderated by staff.

Multi-Tenant Factory Panel

Filament's native multi-tenancy scopes every factory, campaign and lead to the owning company. Platform staff use a separate admin panel.

Arabic-Aware Search

Normalized Arabic text (tashkeel, alef/hamza, taa marbuta) behind MySQL FULLTEXT, with a drop-in Meilisearch driver.

Programmatic SEO

Sector × location landing pages, SEO templates, structured data, sitemaps, slug redirects and indexability scoring.

AI-Assisted Data Pipeline

Queued jobs use AI agents to classify factories into sectors and translate names. They also handle bulk CSV and media imports.

Verification & Leads

Factory applications, document-based verification, reviews, buyer inquiries and lead tracking, all with a full activity log.

Technical Challenges & Solutions

Challenge: One Backend, Many Clients

The web app (and a future Flutter mobile app) needed a stable contract. Meanwhile, staff and factory owners needed rich management screens that must never leak data across tenants.

Solution

A single GraphQL schema is the only public contract. Two Filament panels sit on top of the same Eloquent models, and the tenant is enforced at panel level. The company ID is denormalized onto factories, campaigns and leads so every resource scopes directly. Business rules live in thin resolvers backed by shared services, so search and the auction behave the same everywhere.

Challenge: Fair, Accurate Ad Billing

Pay-per-click billing has to be correct to the piastre and resistant to bot traffic, without slowing down search.

Solution

Money is stored as integer piastres everywhere. Clicks go through signed tracking URLs, and search-volume logging filters out bots before it feeds keyword pricing. The auction ranking is covered by Pest tests.
